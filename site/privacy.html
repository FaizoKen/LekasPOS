LekasPOS privacy policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

LekasPOS is a free, open-source point-of-sale app for small shops (source code, GPL-3.0). This page explains what happens to the information you put into it. In short: your data stays on your phone, and — only if you turn on sync — in your own Google Drive. The developer never receives any of it.

What the app stores

Everything you enter or record while running your shop: products, prices and stock; sales, refunds and payments; suppliers and purchases; staff names, roles and PINs; customers (names, phone numbers, credit balances); shifts, cash movements, settings and an audit log of sensitive actions. PINs are stored only as salted, hashed values, never as the digits themselves.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on the phone. You (the shop) decide what is entered and are responsible for the customer and staff details you record.

What leaves the phone

  • Nothing, by default. The app has no account with us, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting services. It works fully offline.
  • Google Drive backup (optional). If you turn on Google Drive backup (Settings → Google Drive backup) and sign in with a Google account, the app stores its sync files in that account's hidden app data folder in Google Drive, so the shop's tills share the same data. The app asks Google only for this folder (the drive.appdata permission): it cannot see or change any of your other Drive files, and other apps cannot read its folder. The files travel directly between your phones and Google over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The app reads the account's e-mail address only to show you which account is in use. Google's privacy policy applies to data kept in Google Drive. Sign-in uses Google Play services.
  • Daily copy to a folder (optional). If you pick a folder (for example on an SD card or USB drive), the app writes one backup file a day there and keeps the last 7. It stays on that card or drive; nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Files you choose to share. Backup files and CSV exports go only where you send or save them (for example WhatsApp, e-mail, a USB stick or your Drive).

Permissions

  • Bluetooth — to print receipts and read barcode scanners you have already paired in Android settings. The app does not scan for nearby devices or use your location.
  • Camera (optional) — to scan barcodes. Images are processed on the phone and are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Internet and network state — only for Google Drive sync.
  • Keep awake — so backups and sync can finish in the background.

Deleting your data

  • Uninstalling the app, or Android Settings → Apps → LekasPOS → Clear storage, deletes the data on that phone (including the backups kept on it).
  • Turning off sync stops sending data. To delete the synced files, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → LekasPOS → Options → Delete hidden app data.
  • Backup files copied to a folder you picked (SD card, USB drive) are not deleted with the app; delete them in a file manager.
  • Backup and CSV files you saved elsewhere are yours to delete.

Children

LekasPOS is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. The history of every change is public in the source repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue at github.com/FaizoKen/LekasPOS/issues.
