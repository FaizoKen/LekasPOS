LekasPOS

A free, open-source point-of-sale app for small grocery stores and mini markets on Android (5.0 and newer). It works fully offline: sell, print receipts, manage stock, staff and customers, and see reports on the phone. Several tills of one shop can stay in step through the shop's own Google Drive. English and Bahasa Melayu.

Aplikasi jualan (POS) percuma dan sumber terbuka untuk kedai runcit dan pasar mini, dalam Bahasa Melayu dan Inggeris. Berfungsi sepenuhnya tanpa internet.
