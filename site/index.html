LekasPOS

A free, open-source point-of-sale app for small grocery stores and mini markets on Android (5.0 and newer). It works fully offline: sell, print receipts, manage stock, staff and customers, and see reports on the phone. Google Drive backup keeps a copy of the shop's data in its own Google Drive and keeps several tills in step. English and Bahasa Melayu.

Aplikasi jualan (POS) percuma dan sumber terbuka untuk kedai runcit dan pasar mini, dalam Bahasa Melayu dan Inggeris. Berfungsi sepenuhnya tanpa internet.

Install

  1. On the Android phone or tablet, open this page and tap Download LekasPOS.
  2. Open the downloaded LekasPOS.apk. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. If Play Protect warns about an unknown app, tap More details → Install anyway. This is normal for apps that are not installed from Google Play.
  4. Open LekasPOS: the welcome screen sets up the shop. Choose Google Drive backup or a daily copy to an SD card / USB drive, so a lost or broken phone never loses your sales.

Update

From version 1.6.0 the app updates itself: when a new version is out, an Update button appears at the top of the selling screen (and in Settings → App updates). Tap it and confirm when Android asks — your products, sales and settings stay. The first time, Android asks you to allow LekasPOS to install apps. Older versions: download the new version from this page and install it over the old one. Your version is in the app under Settings → About.

Muat turun dan pasang

  1. Pada telefon atau tablet Android, buka halaman ini dan ketik Download LekasPOS.
  2. Buka fail LekasPOS.apk yang dimuat turun. Jika diminta, benarkan pelayar anda memasang aplikasi tidak diketahui.
  3. Jika Play Protect memberi amaran, ketik Butiran lanjut → Pasang juga. Ini biasa bagi aplikasi yang tidak dipasang dari Google Play.
  4. Buka LekasPOS: skrin selamat datang menyediakan kedai anda. Pilih sandaran Google Drive atau salinan harian ke kad SD / pemacu USB, supaya telefon yang hilang atau rosak tidak menghilangkan jualan anda.

Kemas kini: mulai versi 1.6.0 aplikasi mengemas kini dirinya sendiri: apabila versi baharu keluar, butang Kemas kini muncul di atas skrin jualan (dan di Tetapan → Kemas kini aplikasi). Ketik butang itu dan sahkan apabila Android bertanya — produk, jualan dan tetapan anda kekal. Kali pertama, Android meminta anda membenarkan LekasPOS memasang aplikasi. Versi lama: muat turun versi baharu dari halaman ini dan pasang di atas versi lama. Versi anda ada di Tetapan → Perihal.
